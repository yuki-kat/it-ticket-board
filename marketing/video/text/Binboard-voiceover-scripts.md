# Binboard voiceover scripts

Two videos, both 1920×1080 with captions burned in and a silent audio track:

- **Sales video** `Binboard-sales-60s.mp4` (1:00)
- **Open-source showcase** `Binboard-open-source-30s.mp4` (0:34)

**Voice:** warm, confident, conversational; about 2.5 words a second. US or UK English is fine.

**Delivery:** one audio file per line, named as in the **File** column (WAV or M4A, 44.1 or 48 kHz). Alternatively, one take per video read along with the playing video. Leave half a second of silence before and after each line.

Pronunciation: Binboard is "BIN-board". SLA is said as letters, "S-L-A". Ollama is "oh-LAH-mah".
